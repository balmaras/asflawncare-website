What we do

Our Services

From weekly mowing to storm cleanup, we handle the full range of lawn and landscape work your property needs — all from one dependable local team.

Lawn Maintenance

Comprehensive care for your lawn, including mowing, edging, trimming, and fertilization. Whether you're looking for weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly maintenance, we create a customized plan that works for you and keeps your lawn healthy and beautiful year-round.

Sod Installation

We evaluate your site to determine the best sod type and installation method, then handle site prep — removal of old grass and debris, grading, and soil amendments — before laying each piece with precision. You'll get clear watering and maintenance instructions to help your new lawn thrive.

Selected Pruning

We carefully inspect your trees and shrubs and determine the best pruning strategy based on size, shape, and species — removing dead or damaged branches, promoting healthy growth, and shaping plants to complement your landscape design.

Gutter Cleaning

Clogged gutters can lead to serious water damage to your roof, walls, and foundation. We clean out gutters and downspouts, remove leaves and debris, and inspect for signs of damage — so your home stays protected.

Leaf Removal

Prompt, reliable clearing of leaves and debris from your lawn, driveway, walkways, and other outdoor areas — whether you need a one-time seasonal cleanup or ongoing service through fall.

Weed Control

Safe, effective pre-emergent and post-emergent treatment targets unwanted weeds without harming your grass or plants — using advanced techniques and quality products for long-lasting results.

Drainage

We assess water-related problems around your property and identify the source — poor grading, clogged gutters, or inadequate systems — then install French drains, surface drains, and grading solutions to prevent erosion and flooding.

Plant Fertilization

A tailored fertilization plan built around your soil and plant health, using quality fertilizers rich in nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium to strengthen root systems, boost disease resistance, and improve color and appearance.

Storm Debris Hauling

Fast, safe removal of fallen trees, branches, and storm debris — including loading, transport, and environmentally responsible disposal — so your property gets back to normal quickly after severe weather.

Patio Design

From site evaluation and design consultation to material selection and installation, we build custom outdoor living spaces — fire pits, outdoor kitchens, lighting, and more — built to last for years of enjoyment.

Overseed

We spread new grass seed over thinning areas of your existing lawn — caused by weather, foot traffic, or age — using high-quality seed matched to your soil type for even coverage and maximum germination.
